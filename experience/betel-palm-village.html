FAQs

We keep things simple — just what you truly need, and nothing more.  
Here are a few answers to questions guests often ask before arriving among the rice fields.

Is Betel Palm Village suitable for remote work?

Absolutely. Each villa is equipped with a dedicated workspace and ultra-fast Wi-Fi (100 Mbps+), ensuring smooth video calls and uploads. Many of our guests are digital nomads who stay for weeks at a time.

Do all villas have air-conditioning and private bathrooms?

Yes. Every villa features air-conditioning, a private ensuite bathroom, and modern essentials for a comfortable stay.

Is Betel Palm Village pet-friendly?

Yes, we warmly welcome pets. Please inform us in advance if you’re bringing one so we can prepare a suitable villa and space for your furry friend.

How far is it from Pai Walking Street?

We’re located about 2.5 km (5 minutes by motorbike or 10 minutes by car) from Pai Walking Street — close enough for easy access, yet far enough to stay quiet and peaceful.

Is there parking at the property?

Of course! We’re a small retreat with only four villas, so please let us know as soon as possible — we’ll do our best to accommodate you.

Still have questions?

Send us a quick message — we’re just a small team among the rice fields, and happy to help you find the stay that truly fits your heart.

FAQs

We keep things simple — just what you truly need, and nothing more.  
Here are a few answers to questions guests often ask before arriving among the rice fields.

Is there air-conditioning or Wi-Fi in the bungalows?

No air-con and no TV — only a fan and natural wind. Wi-Fi is available in shared areas, but we recommend disconnecting and enjoying the slow rhythm of nature instead.

Do all rooms have private bathrooms?

Some bamboo bungalows share clean, separate bathrooms — while our Wooden Houses come with private ones. If you prefer full privacy, please let us know when booking.

How far is it from Pai Walking Street?

We’re about 2.5 km (10 min by motorbike or 30 min walk) from the main walking street — close enough for a short ride, yet peaceful enough to feel far from everything.

Can I park my motorbike or car at the property?

Yes, free parking is available for both cars and motorbikes.

What’s the best way to get here from Pai bus station?

You can take a local taxi (about 300 THB) or rent a motorbike in town — it’s the easiest way to explore Pai’s beautiful countryside.

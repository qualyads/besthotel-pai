FAQs

We keep things simple — just what you truly need, and nothing more.  
Here are a few answers to questions guests often ask before arriving among the rice fields.

What kind of place is 365 Vila – Connect Garden Pai?

It’s not a luxury villa or a resort — it’s a chill garden house designed for small groups, couples, and pet lovers. Think campfire vibes, BBQ nights, and music under the stars. If you love simple spaces with character, you’ll feel right at home.

Is this place suitable for older guests or families with small children?

Not really. The stairs are metal and a bit steep, and the downstairs area is open and casual — perfect for hanging out, but not for kids running around or elderly guests.

What’s included in the villa?

Two air-conditioned bedrooms upstairs (each with a private bathroom and 6-foot bed), plus an open lounge area downstairs with seating, a fridge, and BBQ zone. The Wi-Fi is fast, but the downstairs space is better for chilling, not working.

Can I work remotely here?

You can — Wi-Fi is strong (100 Mbps+), but the setup is more for relaxation than for all-day work. We’d call it “remote work with mountain air and sunset breaks.”

Is the villa pet-friendly?

Absolutely! We love pets. Please let us know in advance so we can prepare a pet-friendly setup for your stay.

Can we play music or have a BBQ party?

Yes, you can enjoy BBQ and music — just keep the volume moderate after 10 PM to respect our quiet neighborhood. It’s all about fun and good vibes.

Can I request daily cleaning?

Absolutely. Just let us know — our housekeeping team can clean the villa daily upon request.
We’re happy to help keep your space fresh and comfortable throughout your stay.

How far is 365 Vila from Pai Walking Street?

About 5 minutes by motorbike (or a 10–15 minute scenic walk). Close enough for convenience, but far enough for quiet nights.

Is parking available?

Yes, we offer free parking right in front of the villa.

Who is this place perfect for?

People who want freedom, chill vibes, and space to connect. Digital nomads, couples, and small groups who’d rather share stories around a fire than stay in a polished hotel.
